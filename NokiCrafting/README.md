Noki Crafting is an independent addon. Install Auctionator and AHledger separately. Open its auction tab and scan for prices; estimated sales/day come from your own AHledger market data. Automatic scanning is off by default. Use /nccrafttab to open its tab. Recipe knowledge and scans are saved separately from Noki Market. AHledger data must be updated through AHledger sync or /ahl import. Prices from AHledger (ahledger.com).
