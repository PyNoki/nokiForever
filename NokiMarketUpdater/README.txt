NOKI MARKET UPDATER

Requires Windows, Noki Market 1.13.2 or newer, and the AHledger addon.
1. Run Noki Market Updater.exe. Windows may ask for administrator access to write to your addon folder.
2. Choose your region, ruleset, and faction. Select your NokiMarket addon folder if needed.
3. Click Update now, then type /reload in game.
4. Optionally enable updates every 30 minutes while the app remains open. Reload to use each update.

The updater downloads market tables through devvy.xyz from AHledger. Prices from AHledger (ahledger.com). It does not upload your game data and does not modify vendor references or the old history snapshot. It updates daily estimates, but AHledger tables do not include the old 1/2/8/24-hour supply changes. The app is unsigned; Windows may show a warning.
